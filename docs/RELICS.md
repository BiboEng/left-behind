# The 25 relics

**13 on the Moon (Ibrahim) · 12 on Mars (Rayan).** These are leads, not finished facts: every one goes
through `/add-relic` (the `nasa-historian` agent checks official sources and finds NASA photos)
and then **your own check** before `verified: true`. Anything marked **(check)** is a detail to confirm.

★ = suggested hero (gets a NASA 3D model). If NASA 3D Resources has no model for one, swap in a
relic that does. Aim for about 4 heroes per world.

## 🌑 Moon (Ibrahim), 1959 → 2025

| # | Relic | Agency | Arrived (UTC) | Status | Why it's here |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Luna 2 | USSR | 1959-09-13 | impacted | The first human-made object to reach another world |
| 2 | Luna 9 | USSR | 1966-02-03 | retired | First soft landing and first photos from the surface |
| 3 | Surveyor 3 | NASA | 1967-04-20 | abandoned | Visited 2½ years later by Apollo 12, whose crew cut off parts to bring home |
| 4 | ★ Apollo 11 "Eagle" descent stage | NASA | 1969-07-20 | abandoned | The first crewed landing. The flag, laser reflector and memorial items are still there |
| 5 | Lunokhod 1 | USSR | 1970-11-17 | retired | First rover on another world; its exact spot was lost for decades, then found again in 2010 |
| 6 | ★ Apollo 15 "Falcon" + Lunar Roving Vehicle | NASA | 1971-07-30 | abandoned | The first car driven on the Moon, and the "Fallen Astronaut" memorial |
| 7 | ★ Apollo 17 "Challenger" + Lunar Roving Vehicle | NASA | 1972-12-11 | abandoned | The last time humans stood on the Moon |
| 8 | Lunokhod 2 | USSR | 1973-01-15 | retired | Drove about 39 km; sold at auction in 1993, so privately owned while still on the Moon (check) |
| 9 | Lunar Prospector | NASA | 1999-07-31 | impacted | Carried the ashes of Eugene Shoemaker, the only person buried on the Moon |
| 10 | ★ LCROSS + Centaur stage | NASA | 2009-10-09 | impacted | Crashed on purpose into a permanently dark crater, and found water |
| 11 | Vikram (Chandrayaan-2) | ISRO | 2019-09-06 | crashed | Lost during descent; its debris was first spotted by an amateur in NASA LRO images |
| 12 | SLIM "Moon Sniper" | JAXA | 2024-01-19 | retired | A pinpoint landing, but upside down |
| 13 | Resilience (Hakuto-R Mission 2) | ispace | 2025-06-05 | crashed | The most recent arrival: a private lander that couldn't slow down in time |

**Moon outro idea:** nothing has landed since June 2025. China's Chang'e 7, Astrobotic's Griffin and
Blue Origin's Blue Moon have all slipped to 2027 (check before the demo).

## 🔴 Mars (Rayan), 1971 → 2021

| # | Relic | Agency | Arrived (UTC) | Status | Why it's here |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Mars 3 | USSR | 1971-12-02 | lost | First soft landing on Mars. It transmitted for about 20 seconds, then fell silent forever |
| 2 | ★ Viking 1 Lander | NASA | 1976-07-20 | lost | Worked for six years, until a mistaken command from Earth in 1982 cut it off (check) |
| 3 | ★ Mars Pathfinder + Sojourner | NASA | 1997-07-04 | retired | Bounced down on airbags; Sojourner was the first rover to drive on Mars |
| 4 | Mars Polar Lander | NASA | 1999-12-03 | lost | Probably shut off its engines too early, and was never heard from |
| 5 | Beagle 2 | ESA / UK | 2003-12-25 | lost | Lost on Christmas Day, then found 11 years later in NASA HiRISE images |
| 6 | ★ Spirit | NASA | 2004-01-04 | retired | Dragged a broken wheel that dug up signs of ancient hot springs; got stuck in sand |
| 7 | Opportunity | NASA | 2004-01-25 | lost | A 90-day rover that lasted more than 14 years and drove a marathon, until a dust storm in 2018 |
| 8 | Phoenix | NASA | 2008-05-25 | retired | Dug up water ice near the pole, then froze in the Martian winter |
| 9 | Schiaparelli | ESA | 2016-10-19 | crashed | A sensor error made it think it had already landed |
| 10 | ★ InSight | NASA | 2018-11-26 | retired | Recorded over 1,300 marsquakes before dust covered its solar panels |
| 11 | ★ Ingenuity | NASA | 2021-02-18 | retired | The first powered flight on another planet: 72 flights, ended by a damaged rotor in 2024 |
| 12 | Zhurong | CNSA | 2021-05-14 | lost | China's first Mars rover. It hibernated in 2022 and never woke |

**Mars outro idea:** "And two are still working right now: Curiosity and Perseverance." (check current status)
