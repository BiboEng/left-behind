# The relics

**NASA hardware only.** The hackathon challenge is to tell the story of the equipment NASA left on
the Moon and Mars, so every relic is a NASA spacecraft or a piece of one. Do not add Soviet, ESA,
ISRO, JAXA, CNSA or commercial hardware.

**7 on the Moon (Ibrahim) · 12 on Mars (Rayan).** Every relic goes through `/add-relic` (the
`nasa-historian` agent checks official sources and finds NASA photos) and then **your own check**
before `verified: true`.

★ = hero (gets a NASA 3D model). If NASA 3D Resources has no model for one, swap in a relic that
does.

## 🌑 Moon (Ibrahim), 1964 → 2009

| # | Relic | Arrived (UTC) | Status | Why it's here |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Ranger 7 | 1964-07-31 | impacted | After six failed Rangers, the first U.S. close-up pictures of the Moon, sent until impact |
| 2 | Surveyor 1 | 1966-06-02 | retired | The first U.S. soft landing on the Moon |
| 3 | Surveyor 3 | 1967-04-20 | abandoned | Visited 2½ years later by Apollo 12, whose crew cut off parts to bring home |
| 4 | ★ Apollo 11 "Eagle" descent stage | 1969-07-20 | abandoned | The first crewed landing. The flag, laser reflector and memorial items are still there |
| 5 | ★ Apollo 15 "Falcon" + Lunar Roving Vehicle | 1971-07-30 | abandoned | The first car driven on the Moon, and the "Fallen Astronaut" memorial |
| 6 | ★ Apollo 17 "Challenger" + Lunar Roving Vehicle | 1972-12-11 | abandoned | The last time humans stood on the Moon |
| 7 | LCROSS + Centaur stage | 2009-10-09 | impacted | Crashed on purpose into a permanently dark crater, and found water |

## 🔴 Mars (Rayan), 1976 → 2021

| # | Relic | Arrived (UTC) | Status | Why it's here |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | ★ Viking 1 Lander | 1976-07-20 | lost | The first U.S. landing on Mars; worked six years, until a mistaken command in 1982 |
| 2 | Viking 2 Lander | 1976-09-03 | retired | Saw frost on Mars; worked 1,281 Martian days until its batteries failed |
| 3 | ★ Mars Pathfinder + Sojourner | 1997-07-04 | retired | Bounced down on airbags; Sojourner was the first rover to drive on Mars |
| 4 | Mars Polar Lander | 1999-12-03 | lost | Probably shut off its engines too early, and was never heard from |
| 5 | Deep Space 2 (Amundsen and Scott) | 1999-12-03 | lost | Two impactor probes that rode with Mars Polar Lander; never heard from, never found |
| 6 | ★ Spirit | 2004-01-04 | retired | Dragged a broken wheel that dug up signs of ancient hot springs; got stuck in sand |
| 7 | Opportunity | 2004-01-25 | lost | A 90-day rover that lasted more than 14 years and drove a marathon |
| 8 | Phoenix | 2008-05-25 | retired | Dug up water ice near the pole, then froze in the Martian winter |
| 9 | Curiosity's sky crane | 2012-08-06 | impacted | Lowered Curiosity on cables, then flew off to crash on purpose |
| 10 | ★ InSight | 2018-11-26 | retired | Recorded over 1,300 marsquakes before dust covered its solar panels |
| 11 | ★ Ingenuity | 2021-02-18 | retired | The first powered flight on another planet: 72 flights |
| 12 | Perseverance's parachute + backshell | 2021-02-18 | abandoned | The biggest parachute ever opened on Mars, with "Dare Mighty Things" coded into it |
